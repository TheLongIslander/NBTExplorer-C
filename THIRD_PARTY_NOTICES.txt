C-NBT Explorer: bundled Bedrock LevelDB notices
================================================

Desktop builds may statically include the Amulet-Team fork of LevelDB and its
CRC32C, Snappy, Zstandard, and zlib dependencies. The source revisions are
recorded below and in BEDROCK_LEVELDB.md.

Bundled source revisions:

* Amulet-Team/leveldb: 1352243bb27d287b27be94f7591218dddb3ef900
* google/crc32c: 2bbb3be42e20a0e6c0f7b39dc07dc863d9ffbc07
* google/snappy: 6af9287fbdb913f0794d0148c6aa43b58e63c8e3
* madler/zlib: 51b7f2abdade71cd9bb0e7a373ef2610ec6f9daf (v1.3.1)
* facebook/zstd: v1.5.7 release archive, SHA-256
  eb33e51f49a15e023950cd7825ca74a4a2b43db8354825ac24fc1b7ee09e6fa3
